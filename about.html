About Verdant Upholstery Care

WE BELIEVE EVERY FURNITURE PIECE DESERVES BETTER CARE.

Sofas, armchairs, and curtains are the heart of daily living. We founded Verdant Upholstery Care as a specialized furniture-restoration concept focused on textile science rather than generic high-pressure carpet washing.

Our Brand Story

CRAFTED FOR MODERN LIVING & HEIRLOOM COMFORT.

In many homes, a favourite sofa or inherited armchair is replaced prematurely simply because surface spills, pet marks, or traffic dullness make the fabric look tired. Yet beneath the surface layer of embedded dust and oils, the frame and weave remain structurally sound.

Verdant Upholstery Care was designed around a simple principle: treat residential and commercial upholstery with the same respect a textile conservator gives fine drapery. Whether refreshing a contemporary modular sectional or a traditional floral-patterned wingback chair, we calibrate water pressure, temperature, and suction to the exact yarn blend.
