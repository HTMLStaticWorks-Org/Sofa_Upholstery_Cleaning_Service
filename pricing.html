04 • Common Pricing Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED PRICING QUESTIONS.

Everything you need to know about how we estimate and schedule upholstery care.

Sofa cleaning is priced transparently based on seating capacity (2-seater, 3-seater, or multi-piece L-shaped sectional), fabric care protocol, and any optional stain or protective treatments selected. Our online booking calculator provides an immediate sample estimate as you select your items.
Standard synthetic weaves, cotton blends, and microfiber are covered by our base package rates. Delicate cut-pile velvet, natural unbleached linen, or multi-stage hand leather conditioning require specialized solutions and additional technician time, which is clearly itemized before any work begins.
Yes. Booking your sofa set together with dining chairs, armchairs, curtains, or mattresses in a single appointment reduces technician travel overhead and qualifies for our bundled multi-item package rates.
