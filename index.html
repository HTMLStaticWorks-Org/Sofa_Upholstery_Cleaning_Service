The Verdant Standard

CARE THAT GOES BEYOND THE SURFACE.

Household sprays often push dissolved dirt deeper into cushion foam. Our coordinated technicians combine soft-bristle agitation with calibrated commercial vacuum extraction.

1. Professional Cleaning Approach

Commercial injection-extraction units flush suspended soil out of the weave rather than leaving sticky soap residue behind.

2. Fabric-Specific Care

Every appointment begins by checking manufacturer care codes (W, S, W/S, X) and testing dye stability in a hidden seam.

3. Attention to Detail

From button tufts and welt piping to inner frame corners and floor protection mats, we treat your home with respect.

4. Convenient Booking

Select your furniture items, choose a morning, afternoon, or evening slot, and track your request in your Customer Dashboard.
