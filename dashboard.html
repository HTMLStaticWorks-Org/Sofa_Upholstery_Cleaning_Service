Pre-Visit Preparation

HOW TO PREPARE FOR YOUR TECHNICIAN VISIT

01. Clear Walkways & Throw Items

Remove fragile side-table decor and non-upholstered throw blankets around the sofa or curtains being treated.

02. Note Specific Spill Zones

Point out any coffee, wine, food oil, or pet spots during the initial walkthrough so we can pre-treat them first.

03. Allow 2–4 Hours Airflow Drying

Keep normal room ventilation or ceiling fans running after extraction for rapid, even fabric drying.

On-Site Quality Standard

WHAT HAPPENS DURING ON-SITE EXTRACTION

Fibre & Dye Verification

Every appointment starts with a manufacturer tag check (W, S, W/S, X, L) and an inconspicuous seam colourfastness test.

HEPA Vacuum & Pre-Spray

Dry soil and allergens are extracted from deep cushion crevices before eco-conscious enzymatic pre-conditioners are applied.

Clear-Nozzle Rinse & Pile Grooming

Controlled spray-extraction flushes out suspended dirt until rinse water runs clear, followed by directional nap grooming.
